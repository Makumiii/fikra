# Fikra product brief

Fikra is an independently owned, single-author social blog. The author publishes short thoughts and durable articles; readers browse without an account and may subscribe to claim a handle, join conversations, react, bookmark, and manage email preferences.

The owner uses the same application with a server-assigned role. Publishing, moderation, profile editing, subscriber summaries, and analytics remain invisible and inaccessible to readers.

Core principles:

- Public reading without an account wall.
- Passwordless identity through verified email and a public handle.
- Familiar timeline interaction without an algorithmic feed.
- Stable post-ID URLs, strong discovery metadata, and portable content.
- Calm, compact interfaces with progressive disclosure.
- Cloudflare-native infrastructure with explicit backup and recovery paths.

The repository ships generic content. An installation becomes personal only through `/setup` and subsequent owner edits.
